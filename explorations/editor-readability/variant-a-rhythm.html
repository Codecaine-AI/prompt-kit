▶ A — Measure & Rhythm typographic restraint only; the layout is the classic editor

Same surface — gutter, tags, real indentation — with nothing added. Readability comes entirely from a capped measure, leading set per line role, a three-step mono type scale, and blank lines that render at graded heights so vertical rhythm carries the grouping.

  • Cap the measure (typography §9): prose stops at 76ch with hanging indents; no line runs to 136ch.
  • Line-height by role (typography §7): bare tag lines sit at 17px; wrapped prose gets ≥1.6 leading.
  • Fewer sizes, one small scale (typography §4–5): three roles — 13.5px body, 12px structure, 10.5px gutter.
  • Group with space, not lines (layout §1): blank lines render 13 / 22 / 32 / 44px, so inter-section gaps are ≥2× intra-group; zebra and per-line rules deleted.
  • Letter-spacing by size (typography §8): a touch of tracking on the small uppercase chrome and gutter only; none at body size.
  • Font smoothing on the root (typography §17): antialiased once, globally.
